Lee College Counseling Services

How I Can Help

  • Provides confidential, short-term therapeutic services
  • Helps students cope with depression, anxiety, stress, low self-esteem, and related issues
  • Conducts individual and group therapy sessions (in-person or virtual) for students 18 and older
  • Participates in the CARES Team as a collaborator and resource
  • Coordinates mental health education and prevention programming
  • Develops community referral relationships
  • Responds to campus crises as needed
  • Supports Title IX reporting with confidential assistance
  • Promotes TimelyCare utilization for 24/7 student support
  • Maintains confidential records per ethical and licensing standards

TimelyCare

Lee College partners with TimelyCare to provide free, 24/7 telehealth and mental health support for all enrolled students.

📱

Access TimelyCare

Visit timely.care/lee or download the TimelyCare app.

  • Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Free for all currently enrolled Lee College students
  • Talk to a counselor, medical provider, or health coach
  • Completely confidential

CARES Team

The Campus Assessment, Response, and Evaluation (CARES) Team coordinates support for students experiencing difficulties that may affect their safety or academic success.
