Lee College Counseling Services

How I Can Help

  • Provide confidential, short-term therapeutic services
  • Help students cope with depression, anxiety, stress, low self-esteem, and related issues
  • Conduct individual and group therapy sessions (in-person or virtual) for students 18 and older
  • Participate in the CARES Team as a collaborator and resource
  • Coordinate mental health education and prevention programming
  • Develop community referral relationships
  • Respond to campus crises as needed
  • Support Title IX reporting with confidential assistance
  • Promote TimelyCare utilization for 24/7 student support
  • Maintain confidential records per ethical and licensing standards

On-Campus Counseling

Confidential, short-term counseling services are available to all currently enrolled Lee College students (age 18+), both in-person and virtually.

How to Make an Appointment

  • Contact Kimberly Rawlinson-Stevenson directly at 281.427.5661
  • Submit a referral through the CARES Team at lee.edu/behavioral-intervention-team or email bit@lee.edu
  • Visit the Advising and Transfer Center (Rundell Hall, Room 100) — staff can connect you with counseling services
  • Use TimelyCare for immediate 24/7 virtual support at timely.care/lee

Additional On-Campus Support

  • Access Center (Rundell Hall, Room 101) — accommodations and advising for students with disabilities, including psychiatric diagnoses · 281.425.6217 · lee.edu/disabilities
  • Veterans Center (Rundell Hall, Room 108) — support and counseling for veterans and dependents, including PTSD · 832.556.4302 · lee.edu/veterans

🔒 All sessions are strictly confidential.

TimelyCare

Lee College partners with TimelyCare to provide free, 24/7 telehealth and mental health support for all enrolled students.

📱

Access TimelyCare

Visit timely.care/lee or download the TimelyCare app.

  • Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Free for all currently enrolled Lee College students
  • Talk to a counselor, medical provider, or health coach
  • Completely confidential
